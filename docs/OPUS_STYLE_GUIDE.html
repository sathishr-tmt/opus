A brand system for a modern careers platform. Calm, confident, and functional — the interface should recede so the jobs, applications, and people stand out.

The mark is an O — a body of work, an "opus" — with a single line rising up through it: a career on an upward path. Built from one circle and one stroke, so it stays crisp from a 512px app icon to a 28px favicon. Keep clear space around it equal to the ring's width.

Voice & principles

  • Calm over loud. White space and slate text; violet only where it counts.
  • One primary action per screen. Never two violet buttons competing.
  • Sentence case everywhere. "Save and continue", not "Save And Continue".
  • Plain, warm words. "Nothing here yet" beats "No records found".
  • Flat surfaces. No gradients, heavy shadows, or glow — clean cards and hairline borders.
